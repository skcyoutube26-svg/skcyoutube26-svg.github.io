Privacy Policy

Talay YouTube · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the private tool called Talay YouTube handles Google and YouTube data. The tool is used only by Shahriar Kamal Chowdhury to produce private analytics reports for his own YouTube channels.

What data is accessed

Talay YouTube requests read-only access through Google’s official APIs, using these permissions:

  • YouTube Analytics (including monetary analytics where available)
  • Basic YouTube channel and video information needed to read those reports

The tool can only read this information. It cannot upload, edit, comment on, or delete videos, and it cannot change channel settings or account settings.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce private analytics reports for the channel owner. It is not used for advertising, credit decisions, or any other purpose.

How the data is stored and shared

Reports and any copied analytics data are stored privately on systems controlled by the channel owner. This information is not sold, is not shared with third parties, and is not used to train public AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

Talay YouTube’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

You can turn off Talay YouTube’s access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Find the app in the list, open it, and remove its access. After access is removed, the tool can no longer read your YouTube data.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Talay YouTube: skc.youtube26@gmail.com.
